How It Works

Everything you need to know about booking with Elysio Experiences

For Tourists

1

Search Properties

Browse our catalog of verified accommodations around the world. Use filters to find the perfect place by location, price, number of guests, and amenities. You can search without logging in.

2

Book & Pay Fee

When you find the perfect property, select your dates and number of guests. You'll pay a $7 USD booking fee to secure your reservation. This fee is non-refundable. The remainder of your payment will be made directly at the accommodation.

3

Admin Approval

After you book, our admin team reviews your reservation. This typically takes less than 24 hours. We'll let you know by WhatsApp or email once your booking is approved or if there are any issues, and you can check its status in My bookings.

4

Enjoy Your Stay

Once approved, you'll receive check-in details. Pay the remainder of your stay directly at the accommodation. After your stay, leave a review to help other travelers!

For Hosts

1

Register as Host

Create an account and select "List my property" as your role. Our team will review your application and approve your host account.

2

Publish Your Property

Add photos, description, amenities, pricing, and availability for your property. Set your blocked dates for when the property is not available.

3

Get Approved

Our admin team reviews your property listing to ensure it meets our quality standards. Once approved, your property becomes visible to tourists.

4

Receive Bookings

When tourists book your property, you'll be notified. After admin approval, you'll receive the guest's contact information to coordinate check-in details. Payment is collected directly from the guest at the accommodation.

Payment Process

When you make a reservation, you pay a $7 USD fee to secure your booking. The remainder is paid directly at the accommodation.

$7 USD Pre-booking Fee

A $7 USD fee is charged when you make a reservation. This fee:

  • Secures your reservation
  • Is non-refundable
  • Is paid via QvaPay or PayPal
  • Is processed securely during checkout

Remainder Payment

The remaining balance is paid directly at the accommodation, in cash. You have two options:

  • Full payment on arrival: Pay the entire remaining amount when you check in
  • Daily payment: Pay day by day during your stay (flexible option)

Cancellation Policy

Important: The $7 USD booking fee is non-refundable if you cancel or if your booking is rejected for not meeting requirements. Exception: if the rejection is due to our own error, we will arrange a refund with you.

If your booking is rejected by the admin, we will let you know by WhatsApp or email. If the rejection was due to our own error, we will arrange a refund; otherwise, the fee covers the service of processing and reviewing your reservation.
