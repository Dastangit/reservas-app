Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about Elysio Experiences

The booking process is simple:

  1. Search: Browse properties without logging in. Use filters for location, price, guests, and amenities.
  2. Book: Select your dates and pay a $7 USD booking fee via QvaPay or PayPal to secure your reservation.
  3. Approval: Our admin team reviews your booking within 24 hours. We'll let you know by WhatsApp or email, and you can check its status in My bookings.
  4. Stay: Once approved, you receive the host's contact info and check-in details. Pay the remainder directly at the accommodation.

If the admin rejects your booking, we'll let you know by WhatsApp or email with the reason (if provided), and you'll also see it in My bookings. If the rejection is due to our own error, we will arrange a refund of the $7 USD fee. In any other case, the $7 USD booking fee is non-refundable.

You can contact our admin team if you have questions about the rejection.

You can cancel your booking before it is approved by the admin. The $7 USD booking fee is refunded only if the problem is the platform's responsibility (for example, a system error or a duplicate charge).

Once your booking is approved, cancellation terms are between you and the host. We recommend communicating directly with the host to discuss any changes.

When you book, you pay a $7 USD fee via QvaPay (card or balance) or PayPal (manual transfer) to secure your reservation. The remainder is paid directly to the host on arrival.

  • We accept QvaPay (card or balance) and PayPal (manual transfer, no PayPal account required)
  • Choose your preferred method at checkout

The fee is refunded only if the problem is the platform's; in that case we arrange the refund with you. The stay is paid directly to the host, so that money never goes through the platform.

At checkout, choose QvaPay or PayPal and complete the $7 USD payment. The process takes just a few minutes.

To become a host on Elysio Experiences:

  1. Register: Create an account and select "List my property" as your role.
  2. Approval: Our admin team will review your application and approve your host account.
  3. Publish: Once approved, you can publish your property with photos, description, amenities, pricing, and availability.
  4. Go Live: After admin approval of your listing, your property becomes visible to tourists.

There is no fee to list your property. The $7 USD fee is paid by the tourist when they book.
